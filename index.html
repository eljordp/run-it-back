How to play

Run a life from birth to death

  1. Pick or roll a character. Hometown, family, starting class, look. Or click a preset.
  2. Spend yearly moves. Each year you get 2–5 moves. Click an action in any lane. Stats shift. Story builds.
  3. Hit Age Up at the top of the life panel to advance one year. New events fire. NPCs change.
  4. Lanes unlock by age. Little Kid < 8, Teen 12–24, Vices 14+, Politics 18+. Hit "More" to see everything.
  5. You'll die. Old age, OD, jail, crossfire, car accident. The recap screen is the share-worthy moment.

Stuck? Hover any stat for what it does. Cryptic category? Hover its tile. Want a preset run? Pick one in New Life.
